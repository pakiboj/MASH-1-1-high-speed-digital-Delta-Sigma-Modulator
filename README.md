## SRB

Ovaj kod i rad u njemu su moj kompletan diplomski rad sa kojim sam zvanično završio osnovne studije na Elektrotehničkom fakultetu Univerziteta u Beogradu. U radu je detaljno obrađivana tema Delta-Sigma modulatora, dat primer projektovanja jednostavnog modulatora, predložen i obrađen primer optimizovanijeg modulatora za RF upotrebe i obrađena ograničenja za konstruisanje takvog RF uredjaja. 

Hvala profesoru Dušanu Grujiću što me je mentorisao u izradi mog završnog rada.

Ovaj rad, kao moja intelektualna svojina, objavljen je pod licencom -radi šta god hoćeš-. Toliko sam radio šta god hoću da se nisam potrudio ni da stavim licencu.

U direktorijumu `python` izloženi su kodovi korišćeni za projektovanje predloženih modulatora.

U direktorijumu `cpp` nalaze se kodovi korišćeni za C++ simulacije predloženih komponenti. 

U direktorijumu `hdl` nalaze se VHDL kodovi korišćeni za simulacije. Nažalost komponenta nije bila sintetisana i najverovatnije u ovom oblike neće ni biti, ali nadam se da kroz moje dalje studije ću imati priliku da nešto ovakvo spustim na razvojnu ploču.

## ENG

This code and the work contained in this repository constitute my complete Bachelor's thesis, with which I officially completed my undergraduate studies at the School of Electrical Engineering, University of Belgrade. The thesis explores Delta-Sigma modulators in detail, including an example of designing a simple modulator, a proposed and analyzed more optimized modulator for RF applications, and the limitations involved in designing such an RF device.

I would like to thank Professor Dušan Grujić for supervising and mentoring me throughout the development of my thesis.

This work, as my intellectual property, is published under the “do whatever you want” license. I was so committed to doing whatever I wanted that I didn't even bother to specify an actual license.

The `python` directory contains the code used to design the proposed modulators.

The `cpp` directory contains the code used for C++ simulations of the proposed components.

The `hdl` directory contains the VHDL code used for simulations. Unfortunately, the component was never synthesized and most likely will not be in its current form. However, I hope that during my further studies I will have the opportunity to implement something like this on a development board.
